# Third-party materials

The digitized traces under \`reference/\` and the overlays in \`results/\` adapt Figures 5(b) and 6(b) from Tuure Nurminen, Rayane Mourouvin, Marko Hinkkanen and Jarno Kukkola, *Multifunctional Grid-Forming Converter Control Based on a Disturbance Observer*, IEEE TPEL (2024), [DOI 10.1109/TPEL.2024.3433503](https://doi.org/10.1109/TPEL.2024.3433503), under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The plotted coordinates were extracted, aligned in time, and compared with independent simulations.

The optional PWM comparison runs [motulator](https://github.com/Aalto-Electric-Drives/motulator) from its separate upstream repository. Its code is not distributed here.
